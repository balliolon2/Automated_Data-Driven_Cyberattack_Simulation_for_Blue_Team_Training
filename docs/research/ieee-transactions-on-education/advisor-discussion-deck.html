02 · Research Gap — What These Papers Don't Cover

Three findings from screening 30 ToE records. Status: working hypothesis, grounded in abstract-level evidence.

GAP 1

No SOC-analyst decision training. Every SOC / security-operations query returned zero results in the journal — no paper trains log investigation, TP/FP triage, or response selection.

GAP 2

No adaptive scenario generation. The closest neighbor (PTPA, 2022) uses fixed simulation modalities — none generates scenarios adaptively from an individual learner's skill gaps.

GAP 3

Comparative-effectiveness deficit. A 2023 PRISMA review in this journal finds the literature "lacks evaluation of comparative effectiveness" — the exact weakness our pre/post design addresses.

Working hypothesis — not a conclusion
An adaptive, decision-based Blue Team simulation that maps Security+ domain skill gaps to scenario generation — with log investigation, TP/FP classification, response selection, and measurable pre/post assessment — is a publishable contribution to IEEE ToE if the evidence goes beyond self-report.

04 · Evidence Our System Must Produce

What the publication version must demonstrate to meet this journal's evidence bar.

Behavioral pre/post evidence

Pre/post total and per-domain scores under a matched blueprint — mirroring PTPA's covariate design rather than satisfaction surveys.

Scenario-level outcomes

TP/FP accuracy, key-finding discovery and response-action accuracy per scenario, plus participant flow and attrition reporting.

AI governance

Schema-validated LLM scenario generation, prompt/model versioning, and a proven static fallback — the governance layer no screened paper reports.
